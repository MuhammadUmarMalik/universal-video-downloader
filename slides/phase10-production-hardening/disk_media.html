Disk and media defenses contain failure instead of hiding it.

06 / 10
CONTAINMENT
Storage boundary

Capacity is checked twice.

The destination must remain safe before transfer begins and before the next chunk is written.

Fixed headroom reserves space beyond the next write.
Known and unknown responses remain bounded.
Disk-full and permission failures are stable, non-retryable outcomes.
Media boundary

Processing is size- and type-bound.

FFmpeg only receives validated, regular, non-symlinked artifacts within explicit processing limits.

4 GiB ceiling applies to media inputs and processed outputs.
Empty, oversized, and unsafe artifacts fail closed.
Direct arguments, bounded stderr, timeout, cancellation contain process behavior.
FAILURE IS
DURABLE
Atomic finalization prevents a partial success from being presented as a completed download. Errors map to safe, user-visible states without exposing shell or raw process diagnostics.
