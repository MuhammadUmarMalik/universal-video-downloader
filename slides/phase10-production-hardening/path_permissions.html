BOUNDARY
Intake gate

Only safe destinations enter the pipeline.

Roots must be absolute, normalized, and free of parent traversal. Candidate paths remain inside the selected root.

REJECTED: separators · control characters · reserved names · invalid filesystem characters · temporary suffixes · trailing dots/spaces · . / ..
01

Private artifacts

Application data is 0700; SQLite, partial, processing, finalized, and recovered media artifacts are hardened to 0600 on Unix.

02

Symlink exclusion

Roots, inputs, partials, outputs, and final files use symlink metadata checks before read, write, delete, or rename.

03

Trusted executable

FFmpeg must be a regular executable; group- or world-writable binaries are rejected before process launch.

OWNERSHIP RULE
The browser never performs filesystem or process work. It submits typed requests to the Rust boundary.
